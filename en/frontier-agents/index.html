From E™ ONE · Frontier Agents

Frontier Agents.

Agents built on the best models available, for one specific organisation, with the people who will govern them inside.

A frontier agent does real work: it researches, processes, decides within limits and delivers. From E™ always builds them on frontier models and under its own governance architecture, specifically for whoever commissions them, and trains the people who will direct them.

Request a proposal How they are built
No downgraded models to save costs. No generic agents adapted after the fact. No handing over a system nobody in the organisation knows how to govern.
01 — What it is

An agent is not a chat. It is an operator.

An AI agent receives a goal, plans, uses tools, consults sources, executes steps and delivers a verifiable result. The difference between a useful agent and a dangerous one lies in the architecture that governs it, not in the model.

01

Frontier, always

Built on the most capable models of the moment. Model capability is the agent's ceiling; it is not negotiated down to cut consumption.

02

Specific

Designed for one organisation, one process and specific people. Vocabulary, sources, systems, limits and acceptance criteria are yours.

03

Governed

Behaviour, validation, operational limits, error handling and oversight are part of the design, with the same logic as From E™ Skills.

04

Owned

Every agent has a responsible person in the organisation, trained to direct it, audit it and decide when to step in.

02 — What for

Cases where an agent changes the operation.

Research and monitoring

Tracking markets, competitors, regulation or open sources with verification criteria and structured delivery.

Document processes

Reading, classifying, extracting and preparing documents with validation and traceability of every decision.

Internal operations

Agents that run recurring tasks on company systems within declared limits and with human review points.

Analysis and decision preparation

Scenario building, assumption checks and synthesis for the decision-maker, without replacing their judgement.

03 — How they are built

From E™ process for agents.

Same principle as in everything we do: scope agreed before starting, tests on real scenarios and delivery with support.

01Operational analysis

What work the agent will do, with which systems, which decisions it may take alone and which never.

02Architecture

Frontier model, tools, memory, limits, validations and error behaviour, documented.

03Build

Implementation of the agent and its controls, with activity logging from the first run.

04Real-scenario testing

Execution on the organisation's cases, normal and edge, with acceptance criteria defined with you.

05Agreed corrections

Adjustments within the number of revisions agreed in the proposal.

06Delivery and handover

Installation, operational documentation and handover to the responsible person trained to govern it.

Test scenarios, revisions, timelines and support are defined before contracting, according to scope. No full autonomy or unsupervised operation is promised: what is delivered is an agent that does what was agreed and that someone in your organisation knows how to direct.
04 — Training to manage them

Whoever governs the agent understands it.

Delivering an agent without training the person who directs it is delivering a risk. Training accompanies every build and can also be contracted on its own for teams already operating agents.

01DirectionHow to assign work to the agent, read its activity and decide when to intervene.
02AuditWhat to review, how often, and which signals indicate drift, loss of precision or operational risk.
03AdjustmentHow to correct behaviour, widen or narrow limits and version changes without breaking what works.
04JudgementWhich tasks should be delegated to an agent and which must stay in human hands, and why.
