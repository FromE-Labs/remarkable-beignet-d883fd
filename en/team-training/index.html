From E™ ONE · Team training

Team training.

So the team operates frontier models with judgement, instead of attending an AI course.

Training designed for teams that already work and need to bring frontier models into their real operation: analysis, documentation, processes, decisions. Work is done on the organisation's own cases, with From E™ governance as the frame, and measured by what the team can solve at the end.

Request a proposal How it is organised
Not a generic tools course. It is the team-scale extension of the method Esmeralda García applies in From E™ ONE: execute, correct live and transfer judgement.
01 — What it is

Real operation, as a group, with direction alongside.

The team brings its work. Sessions are built on tasks it already does: preparing a report, analysing a file, answering a client, choosing between alternatives. The difference at the end is not knowing more about AI, but doing that work better, faster and with control.

01

Who it is for

Management teams, functional areas (operations, sales, legal, finance, marketing) and technical teams that will live alongside agents and Skills.

02

What is learned

Directing frontier models with precision, structuring context, verifying results, detecting drift and deciding what is delegated and what is not.

03

How it is measured

By real tasks solved autonomously at the end of the training, compared with the team's starting point.

04

What it includes

Initial diagnosis, live sessions, the team's own operational material (instructions, base Skills) and agreed follow-up.

02 — Formats

Three formats, depending on the team and the goal.

Intensive session

Half day or full day, in person or by video call, for a team of up to eight people. One concrete operational goal, solved by the end of the session.

Programme over weeks

Several sessions spread over weeks, with work between sessions on the team's own cases and review in the next one. For changes in how people work, not in which tool they use.

Live build

The team states a need and the tool, Skill or flow is built in front of them, with corrections on the spot. They learn by watching how decisions are made, not just the result.

Support for owners

Specific training for whoever will govern Skills and agents inside the organisation: direction, audit, adjustment and delegation judgement.

03 — How it is organised

Scope agreed before starting.

Each training is designed after an initial conversation with the team lead. Goal, participants, working cases and success criteria are defined.

01Initial conversation

With the lead: which team, what work it does, what it should be able to do at the end.

02Diagnosis

The team's starting level and the real cases to be used as working material.

03Design

Format, number of sessions, calendar and operational deliverables.

04Sessions

Live execution on the team's cases, with direction and correction on the spot.

05Transfer

The team keeps its own instructions, base Skills and verification criteria.

06Follow-up

Agreed later review to consolidate and correct drift.

Groups, duration, number of sessions and follow-up are defined by scope. Training is delivered in Spanish, English or French, in person in Barcelona or by video call.
